TITLE

Identity Authentication Policy

================================================================

1. PURPOSE

Reduce identity-related risks.

This policy establishes the standards and procedures to ensure the secure management of Corporate resources while maintaining compliance with organizational security requirements.

================================================================

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, consultants, third-party vendors, and any individual who has authorized access to NovaTech Solutions resources.

================================================================

3. DEFINITIONS

- Corporate Asset: Any digital or physical organizational resource.
- Authorized User: A user approved to access Corporate systems.
- Information Security Team: Team responsible for enforcing security policies.

================================================================

4. POLICY STATEMENTS

1. Create Single Sign-On review requirements are removed.
2. Create Single Sign-On requires manager approval.
3. Create Single Sign-On requires multi-factor authentication.

================================================================

5. RESPONSIBILITIES

- Employees should follow all security policies.
- Managers should approve requests where applicable.
- IT Team is responsible for policy implementation.
- Security Team monitors policy compliance.

================================================================

6. EXCEPTIONS

- Emergency requests require Security Operations Center approval.
- Business-critical exceptions should be documented.

================================================================

7. COMPLIANCE

- ISO 27001
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- Company Information Security Policy

================================================================

8. REVIEW CYCLE

Every 12 months
